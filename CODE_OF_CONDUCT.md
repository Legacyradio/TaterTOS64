# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the TaterTOS64v3 project to be a respectful and
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and other
conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
project maintainer at **legacyindiesubmissions@gmail.com**. All complaints will
be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
