in-OS CPython 3.14 (python3.fry) in TaterTOS. Production-grade. No 3.13 downgrade, no
"lighter path". Every compiled dependency statically linked as a CPython BUILTIN
(the freestanding loader is static-only: Modules/dynload_stub.c — there is no dlopen).

MECHANISM (established): Modules/config.c declares `extern PyObject* PyInit_<m>(void);`

  Python3.mk link. Precedent in-tree: sqlite3, _ssl. THIS is how every extension
  below gets linked in.
