  Chromium build config change!
  Added -U__STDCPP_FLOAT16_T__ -U__STDCPP_FLOAT32_T__ -U__STDCPP_FLOAT64_T__
         -U__STDCPP_BFLOAT16_T__ -U__STDCPP_FLOAT128_T__ to cflags_cc.
  Our C++ shim (<cfloat> → <cstdio> → <type_traits>) triggers GCC 15 to
  define these C++23 stdfloat macros, which enables fast_float's
  std::float16_t/float32_t/float64_t/bfloat16_t code paths. We don't have
  <stdfloat> or those types. Undefining them tells fast_float the truth.
  This REQUIRED gn gen — config change in BUILD.gn. Triggered full rebuild
  (31,824 targets).
