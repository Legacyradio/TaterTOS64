Fixed the Chromium HTTP auth Kerberos/GSSAPI platform gate for TaterTOS.

What changed:
- net/features.gni now excludes is_tatertos from use_kerberos.
- This also disables use_external_gssapi for the TaterTOS target, so Chromium
  does not compile the POSIX GSSAPI path that requires <gssapi.h>.

Why:
TaterTOS does not currently provide a Kerberos/GSSAPI userspace ABI, headers,
configuration, or credential store. Compiling Chromium's POSIX GSSAPI backend
would be a fake Linux/POSIX shortcut. The correct architecture is to leave
Kerberos disabled for TaterTOS until the OS has a real native or intentionally
ported GSSAPI implementation.
