Problem:
GCC, under -Werror, rejected Skia's raw [[clang::reinitializes]] annotations
because the Clang-scoped attribute is ignored by GCC and treated as an error.

Fix:
Added a SK_REINITIALIZES macro in SkTemplates.h that expands to
[[clang::reinitializes]] only when compiling with Clang and the attribute is
reported as supported. Other compilers see an empty annotation.

Why:
This preserves the original annotation for supported Clang builds and avoids
presenting a Clang-only attribute to the TaterTOS GCC build. It is a narrow
third-party portability guard, not a global warning suppression or OS shortcut.
