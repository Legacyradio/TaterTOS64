Fix: addressed IPC enum traits type-limits compiler failure.

Files:
- chromium-tatertos/src/ipc/param_traits_macros.h

Why:
- The build stopped on -Werror=type-limits in the expansion of IPC_ENUM_TRAITS_MAX_VALUE (which uses IPC_ENUM_TRAITS_MIN_MAX_VALUE with minvalue=0). When the enum's underlying type is unsigned, comparing 'value >= 0' is always true, triggering the warning.
- Resolved by casting operands to int64_t in the validation expression, ensuring the comparison is safe and doesn't trigger the always-true warning for unsigned types.
- Classification: compiler strictness/library analysis, not a TaterTOS API/platform gap.
