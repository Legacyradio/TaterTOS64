Fry Log 1263 — ui/aura: dangling-else braces
Timestamp: 2026-05-18 ~22:20 CDT

Scope:
  Build stopped at [385/20635] with -Wdangling-else in two ui/aura files.

Files fixed:
  1. ui/aura/client/drag_drop_client.cc:25
  2. ui/aura/client/event_client.cc:24

  Pattern: if (root_window) DCHECK_EQ(...); return root_window ? ...
  GCC 15 + -Werror=dangling-else requires braces to avoid ambiguous else.

Fix:
  Added braces around the if body in both files.

Why:
  GCC 15 -Wdangling-else warns when an if-without-braces is followed by
  another statement that could be parsed as an else body. Adding braces
  resolves the ambiguity.
