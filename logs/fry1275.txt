Fry Log 1275 — build/config/tatertos/BUILD.gn: suppress __STDCPP_FLOAT*_T__
Timestamp: 2026-05-19 ~14:05 CDT

Scope:
  Build stopped at [35/19529] with cascading errors in fast_float
  type traits. Root cause: our C++ shim includes <cfloat> which triggers
  GCC 15 to define __STDCPP_FLOAT16_T__, __STDCPP_FLOAT32_T__,
  __STDCPP_FLOAT64_T__, __STDCPP_BFLOAT16_T__, and __STDCPP_FLOAT128_T__
  (C++23 standard floating-point type macros). These enable fast_float's
  std::float16_t/float32_t/float64_t code paths, but we don't have
  <stdfloat> or those types in our freestanding libc.

Chromium changes:
  build/config/tatertos/BUILD.gn
    - Added -U flags to cflags_cc to undefine all 5 stdfloat macros.

Why:
  Root-cause fix. Without these undefines, fast_float's is_supported_float_type
  template inheritance chain breaks because std::is_same<T, std::float64_t>
  fails (float64_t doesn't exist in our libc++). This cascade manifests as
  "'value' is not a member" or "template argument N is invalid" errors
  depending on instantiation order.

Requires gn gen (config change in BUILD.gn).
