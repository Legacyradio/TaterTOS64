---------------------------------------------------------------------
CONCLUSION
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The old /dev/urandom-triggered JSC/libuv crash is fixed far enough that
it no longer appears during two boots. Claude launches successfully as
pid 12 and the system continues through the existing probe suite.

However, Claude still does not print --version and does not fault before
timeout. The 900-second run produced the same 207-line serial shape as
the 420-second run. That means the next symptom is a silent wait/hang or
long-running path after Claude launch, not the previous immediate
0xBBADBEEF crash.

The older fry logs already warned that the next likely rungs after
startup are:

  - real signal delivery / signal setup
  - JIT executable mappings / RWX mmap and mprotect behavior
  - longer libc/runtime assumptions
  - sockets/TLS later for full functionality, but not required for
    claude --version
