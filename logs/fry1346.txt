fry1346 - fix sparse reservation threshold: only >= 64 MiB
timestamp: 2026-06-04 11:55:00 CDT

What changed:
- src/kernel/proc/syscall.c: tightened the sparse_reserve condition.
  Old: anon_private AND (MAP_NORESERVE OR >= 64MB OR >= 4GB)
       → ANY MAP_NORESERVE mapping was sparse, even 4 KB.
  New: anon_private AND >= 64 MiB AND (MAP_NORESERVE OR >= 4 GiB)
       → only large regions are sparse; small NORESERVE gets eager pages.

Why:
- The old condition was changed in a previous fry log to fix gigacage
  reservations, but it was too broad. Every single MAP_NORESERVE mmap
  (including 4 KB ones) was treated as sparse, meaning no pages were
  committed. Bun immediately dereferences the returned pointer and
  gets a page fault with no page table entry.
- The new threshold (64 MiB minimum for sparse) ensures small utility
  mappings are eagerly backed while cage-sized reservations (128 MB+)
  remain lazy.

Expected effect:
- The 4 KB mmap at 0x6fdff7a3a000 just before the int3 crash should
  now get an eager page mapping instead of a sparse reservation.
- This may resolve the int3 assertion if it was triggered by Bun
  accessing unmapped NORESERVE memory.
