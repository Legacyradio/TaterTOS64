What changed:
- Added Linux syscall 217 (`getdents64`) to Tater Bridge.
- Added Linux dirent64 encoding for FD_DIR handles.
- Added per-directory fd offset tracking through shared->fd_table[fd].
  This avoids colliding with O_CLOEXEC/O_DIRECTORY bits in fd_flags.
- Added getdents64 to the Claude syscall trace name/filter.

  0x40000000 fault.

  Linux ABI surface fix instead of inventing a new filesystem path.
