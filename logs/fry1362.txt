- New /proc nodes not accessed before assertion fires.
- CR2=0x6FDDEB21E000 — gigacage base shifted by one page vs prior boots
  (different ASLR/mm timing).

Analysis of int3 register state across boots:
  rax = gigacage base (varies: 0x6fddeb22e000 or 0x6fddeb21e000)
  rdx = 0x0000FFFFFFFFFFFF (48-bit canonical mask)
  r9  = 0xFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFC (-4, alignment mask)
  r12 = 0xA (10)
  r14 = 0x6FDFF7A2A030 (another JSC mmap region)
  
  JSC appears to validate that the gigacage base address satisfies
  certain masking constraints. The two gigacage regions are at unrelated
  addresses (0x6fddeb... and 0x6fdff7...) — on real Linux, JSC
  allocates a single huge region and subdivides it with known offsets.
  Our independent mmap placement breaks the relative offsets.

Next step:
- Investigate whether forcing coniguous allocation or honoring mmap
  hints more aggressively would satisfy JSC's addressing constraints.
