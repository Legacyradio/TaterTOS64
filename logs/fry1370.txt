Conclusion:
- The adjacent e8c8/e880 100 ms parking-lot futex pattern is normal Linux
  behavior and is not enough by itself to explain the hang.
- The bug is not prompt/auth/network specific. `claude --version` hangs before
  output under Tater Bridge, while host Linux exits immediately.
- Current narrowed blocker: base Bun/JSC/CLI startup liveness after the first
  normal parking-lot timeout. Most likely areas are clone3/TLS/child_tid or
  parent_tid handoff, futex wake value/state transitions, or a timer/eventfd
  readiness path before CLI output.
