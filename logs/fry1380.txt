WHERE rdi COMES FROM (disasm of caller 0x4C0D1C0..0x4C0D208)
  It is a JSC open-addressing hash probe with 6-BYTE PACKED entries:
    idx = (hash+probe) & mask(r15d); entry = r13 + idx*6
    rdi = (movzwl entry+4 << 32) | (mov entry+0)   ; reconstruct 48-bit pointer
    if (rdi==0) empty/not-found; if (rdi==1) deleted/skip; else string-compare.
  The bad entry: low32=0x00000000, high16=0xC200 -> rdi=0xC20000000000. So an
  entry's high-16 field holds 0xC200 where an empty entry should be all-zero.
  Then 0x4C17270 (string/atom equality: len@+4, data@+8) derefs rdi -> #GP.
