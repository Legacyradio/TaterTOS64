NEW BLOCKER: demand stack-growth #PF
  vec=0x0E (#PF) at CR2=0x00007FFFFF7FFFF8 on pid=3 (main thread). That is 8 bytes
  below 0x7FFFFF800000 — the main thread's stack grew ~8MB down and faulted just
  below its mapped region. Linux auto-grows the stack on a fault just below the
  stack VMA (up to RLIMIT_STACK); we do not, so demand-paging (which only commits
  WITHIN an existing region) doesn't cover it -> kill. Bun's crash handler then
  prints panic + UD2 (0f0b).
