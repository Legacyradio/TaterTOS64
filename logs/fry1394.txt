WHY IT'S NOT FULLY FIXED (sharpened root cause)
  The GC thread (pid 3) sends its suspend signal sig 30 ONLY to pid 4 and pid 8
  (52x/2x), NEVER to pid 2 — confirmed again this boot. pid 2 (the main mutator
  AND the crashing thread) is only ever at a safepoint when it happens to be
  inside a blocking syscall (epoll/futex). epoll now blocks, so it parks far
  more, but ANY userspace interval between syscalls that overlaps a pid-3 sweep
  can still let pid 2 read/build an object the collector is recycling.
  Two non-exclusive explanations for "pid 2 never signaled":
   (a) JSC skips signaling threads it believes are already parked; a race
       between that check and pid 2 leaving its syscall.
   (b) thread-id mapping: JSC tgkill()s a tid that our kernel resolves to the
       wrong pid (it always lands on 8/4). Verify gettid()/tgkill tid->pid
       mapping for the main thread.
