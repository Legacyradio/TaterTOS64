WHERE THIS LEAVES IT (the actual frontier)
  A specific JS function (same one across versions; invoked from an event-loop
  callback shortly after networking init where socket()=-ENOSYS) has its
  UnlinkedCodeBlock numCalleeLocals=0. The CodeBlock copy at 0x4C84[CF]308
  (mov 0x14(%r8),%edx; and; mov %edx,0x14(%rdi)) faithfully copies that 0; the
  JIT prologue then computes a 0-size frame and walks the stack off. Since
  loader/data is exonerated, the UnlinkedCodeBlock is GENERATED empty: either
  (a) parsed from a source whose (start,end) length our env yields as 0, (b) a
  builtin whose generation short-circuits, or (c) the function is ENTERED while
  its CodeBlock is still an un-filled stub (a tiering/linking ordering our env
  breaks). socket()=-ENOSYS likely schedules the error-path callback that is
  this function.
