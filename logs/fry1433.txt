THE STALL, PINNED ONE LAYER DEEPER
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Timeline: last data frame at serial line 3890; claude reads everything
(recvfrom rc=0x2fff on fd 0x13 visible); then the TBFULL syscall trace
shows the loop: 1,042 epoll_pwait2 calls, 1,037 returning rc=1 — one
ready event, identical, forever — plus 903 clock_gettime, 108
sched_yield, 79 futex, and only SIX recvfrom and ONE read(8 bytes,
fd 15) in the whole window. Level-triggered readiness nobody consumes
= the event loop spins; the next work item (host fry1430 reference:
OPEN MORE CONNECTIONS, proceed to session init) never runs.

The stuck fd is NOT a socket: LXEPRDY prints UNCONDITIONALLY for every
FD_SOCKET ready event (verified in lx_epoll wait loop, syscall.c
~4131) yet printed only 10 times total against 1,037 rc=1 returns.
Socket readiness itself is computed honestly (poll_check_fd:
tcp_rx_available()>0, not sticky). Remaining suspects, in order:
  1. EVENTFD with a counter that never reaches a reader. Two
     eventfd-style fds visible: 6x 8-byte writes to fd 16, 6x to fd 7,
     but only ONE 8-byte read (fd 15) in the entire stall window. A
     counter>0 eventfd registered on epfd 14 reports POLLIN forever
     (poll_check_fd FD_EVENTFD branch) — exactly matches eternal rc=1.
     claude is Bun-based (see fry1393 note in syscall.c); Bun's loop
     wakeup is an eventfd; threads share one fd table (tgid 3 — pid 8
     closed UDP fd 17, pid 9 reused it for TCP).
  2. TIMERFD expirations never cleared by a read.
  3. A pipe read-end with residue.
Cannot name the fd from this log: LXEPCTL and LXEPRDY are both
FD_SOCKET-filtered (the registration of the stuck fd was never even
logged), and the TBFULL trace budget ([2154]..[1]) exhausted mid-stall.

WHY THE CODE WAS NOT CHANGED THIS STEP (rule 6)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
No fix candidate is proven yet; changing eventfd/epoll semantics on a
hypothesis violates the discipline. The fry1434 step is surgical beacon
widening only:
  - LXEPCTL: log ALL fd kinds (with kind), not just FD_SOCKET.
  - LXEPRDY: log ALL fd kinds with kind tag; rate-limit (first 32, then
    every 256th) so a 1,000-spin stall can't flood serial; for
    FD_EVENTFD include the live counter value.
  - Eventfd read/write beacons: fd, value, counter-after.
Then one boot names the stuck fd, its kind, and why its readiness never
clears. That is the fry1435 run.
