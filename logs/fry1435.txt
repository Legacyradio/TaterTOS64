CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The fry1433 busy-spin is fully explained: level-triggered re-reporting
of an edge-registered, deliberately-never-drained wakeup eventfd. The
guest's event loop consumed phantom wakeups instead of parking, so the
