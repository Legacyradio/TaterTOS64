    TBCOPYFD fd=19-24 kind=4 dom=1 (AF_UNIX)  -> copied

CONCLUSION: SIGSEGV scales with copying the AF_UNIX socketpairs
themselves (fry1470=0 segfaults/no copy; fry1471=496/copy-all;
fry1472=717/copy-AF_UNIX-only). AF_UNIX socketpairs in TaterTOS are
PIPE-BACKED ("AF_UNIX pairs over pipe buffers" — g_pipes via
tcp_handle/listen_handle). My socket-level refcount did NOT manage the
underlying g_pipes readers/writers, so sharing a socketpair end across
processes corrupts pipe accounting -> claude faults.

PROPER NEXT APPROACH (for a fresh session, do it carefully + test):
Treat an AF_UNIX socketpair end like FD_PIPE in copy_vfork_fd_table —
i.e. bump the BACKING g_pipes[tcp_handle] and g_pipes[listen_handle]
reader/writer counts (the two directions) when copying to the child, and
ensure lx_close_fd's socketpair path decrements them symmetrically. The
socket struct itself can stay shared but its lifetime must follow the
pipe refcounts, not a separate socket refcount. Verify: no SIGSEGV, main
result prints, AND child rg dup2 works + exits clean.
