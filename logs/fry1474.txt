fry1474 - process-exit AF_UNIX socketpair cleanup fix
timestamp: 2026-06-13T13:18:04-05:00

What changed:
- Patched src/kernel/proc/process.c shared_release() socket cleanup.
- Process exit now honors fry_socket refs before tearing a socket down.
- AF_UNIX socketpairs are released by decrementing their backing pipe
  reader/writer counts instead of calling tcp_close() on tcp_handle and
  listen_handle.
- Real non-AF_UNIX SOCK_STREAM sockets still close through tcp_close().

Why:
- The fry1474 serial log showed the child inherited AF_UNIX socketpair fds,
  dup2'd them onto stdio, failed execve, and exited 127.
- shared_release() then treated the child socketpair pipe indexes as TCP
  connection handles. Pipe indexes 0/1 overlapped live claude TLS TCP
  connections, so the child exit sent FIN/RST on conn 0/1.
- claude then received HUP events on those live epoll fds and crashed with
  the old sig=11/RIP=0 path.
