  fry1483   (16-slot): "countFilesRoundedRg failed" x1  (EBADF then ENFILE)
  fry1484c  (64-slot): "countFilesRoundedRg failed" x0  + 0 EBADF/ENFILE

=> With 64 socket slots, rg's child spawn SUCCEEDS. Both rg blockers are
   gone. They were ONE root cause: socket-pool starvation.
     - ENFILE: socketpair() (nr=53) itself failed at sock=15/16.
     - EBADF : copy_vfork_fd_table (process.c:519-524) skips the child's
       AF_UNIX socketpair-stdio copy when the pool is full (nsi<0 ->
       continue), so the rg child's posix_spawn dup2 hits a missing fd.
   64 slots give room for BOTH the socketpair AND the child copy.

THE "HANG" WAS RATE LIMITING (not the kernel)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
All post-fry1483 runs stalled BEFORE session init with the main thread
(pid 6) parked on a 120ms-timeout futex and a worker (pid 14) polling.
Root cause: HTTP 429 from api.anthropic.com — serial 429 counts:
  fry1484c=176, s_rg_a1=37, s_rg_a4=45.
Token: tier=default_claude_ai, sub=pro. 7 in-OS claude sessions fired in
~1h tripped the Pro rolling-window rate limit; claude's worker retries
429 with backoff -> looks like a pre-init hang. fry1483 (the FIRST run,
fresh quota) completed; everything after progressively 429'd. The 16-vs-
64 correlation was CONFOUNDED with run-order/quota, not caused by slot
count. A wrong socketpair-output/HUP theory was investigated and dropped
once the 429s were found.
LESSON (memory): do NOT machine-gun in-OS claude runs — each is a real
authenticated Pro session; ~4-5/hour and you 429. Space them out, or the
next person will misread the 429 backoff as a kernel hang.
