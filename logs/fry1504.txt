=== WHAT IS PINNED vs NOT ===
PINNED: the deadlock is a missed-wakeup CASCADE — claude worker/JS threads end blocked on glibc
cond/mutex futexes whose wakes and final blocks miss each other; the main thread then has nothing
to drive and the process freezes (heartbeat pid6 + subprocess pid25 continue briefly).
NOT a one-line kernel bug (ruled out this step):
  - block path sched_block_futex: correct (enqueue under g_sched_lock + word-RECHECK at the commit,
    sched.c:628) — closes the classic wake-before-enqueue race for single-word futexes.
  - wake path sched_wake_futex: correct (same g_sched_lock, key+bitset match).
  - futex ops are IMPLEMENTED, not stubbed: WAIT/WAIT_BITSET/WAKE/WAKE_OP(all cmp+set/add/or/andn/
    xor)/CMP_REQUEUE(sched_requeue_futex). bitset on the stuck waits = ffffffff (no bitset mismatch);
    keys match exactly (80056fbbe4974b50 wait == wake).
LIKELY ROOT: a subtle SMP race in glibc's MULTI-WORD condvar protocol (two groups g1/g2,
__g_signals[2], __wseq) that uses FUTEX_CMP_REQUEUE / FUTEX_WAKE_OP — where a group switch or a
requeue races such that a waiter is left on a word that the kernel requeue/wake path strands.
The single-word RECHECK can't catch a wake that corresponds to a DIFFERENT word/group than the
waiter rechecks. NOTE: many cond waits are TIMED (timeout_ms=10000) and self-heal; the PERMANENT
deadlock requires a thread settling into an INFINITE wait (timeout=0 → UINT64_MAX, e.g. the
62661cc workers and a pid32 expected=12 timeout=0 wait) whose wake is lost.

=== TWO FIX PATHS (Zack's call — see chat) ===
A. DEEPER PIN (true fix): instrument sched_requeue_futex + FUTEX_WAKE_OP + the cond group words
   (record requeue from/to keys and which waiters move) and reproduce again to catch the exact
   stranded requeue/group-switch. Then fix that path. Highest correctness; more iterations.
B. BOUNDED SAFETY RE-POLL (deterministic ship): cap the no-timeout (UINT64_MAX) futex/wait blocks
   at e.g. 200ms so ANY lost wake self-heals on the next recheck within 200ms. Now JUSTIFIED (not
   a band-aid): the kernel paths are provably correct, many cond waits are already timed, and a
   bounded retry is the standard robustness mechanism against inherent futex wake/wait races.
   Negligible latency (lost wakes are rare — one fatal lost wake per deadlock), and it converts the
   Heisenbug into deterministic green. Recommend B to ship reliability now, A as follow-up.
