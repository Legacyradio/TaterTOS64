fry1511 - keep serial diagnostics off the desktop terminal

Root cause:
- After restoring framebuffer console output, the old Tater Bridge diagnostic stream showed up on the visible desktop.
- The immediate reason was that kprint_serial_only() still called kprint_string(), which writes to both serial and framebuffer.

Change:
- src/kernel/kprint.c now makes kprint_serial_only() write only to the UART.
- User stdout/stderr still reaches the framebuffer through kprint_write(), so shell and Claude output remain visible on the desktop.

Why:
- The user-facing desktop terminal should not be flooded by TBCLK/TBRIDGE serial diagnostics.
- Serial diagnostics can remain available without taking over the GUI session.
