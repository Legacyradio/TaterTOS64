  Claude Code's interactive UI (Ink/React) requires a terminal: it calls

  returning ENOTTY and /dev/tty absent, isatty()/raw-mode setup fails, so Claude
  falls back to a non-interactive write-and-exit path and terminates.
  Secondary: rename (nr=82) returning -5 broke the atomic .claude.json write.
