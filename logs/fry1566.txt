---------------------------------------------------------------------------------

workers it is executing USERSPACE code that makes no syscalls. Two remaining possibilities:

      GC scanner hot) + workers and does not give pid=14 CPU to finish its turn.
Distinguishing requires seeing whether pid=14 is RUNNING (consuming a CPU; if so, at what userspace RIP) vs not run.
