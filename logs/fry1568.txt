---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE BUG IN OUR OWN INSTRUMENTATION
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- src/kernel/proc/sched.c:38  #define TB_TRACE_CLAUDE_TGID 3u        <-- HARDCODED 3
- src/kernel/proc/syscall.c:158 #define TB_TRACE_CLAUDE_TGID g_tb_trace_claude_tgid  <-- DYNAMIC (=claude's real tgid, 14)
=> All syscall.c beacons (TBFUTEX, TBPROCIN, TBSIG, TBEPW, TBFD0, TBTRACE) correctly traced claude (tgid=14): those
   conclusions STAND. But every sched.c-side trace (the TBCLK t3 census; TBSCHED signal-wake/futex-wake) used tgid=3,
   i.e. some OTHER process (likely init/shell). The t3=1/0/0/0 in fry1567 = process 3's thread count, irrelevant to
   claude. fry1567's "main thread is a userspace spinner (confirmed)" is therefore UNPROVEN. Retract it.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WE STILL RELIABLY KNOW (from the correct tgid=14 syscall.c beacons)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- pid=14 (main thread) makes its LAST syscall at serial line 20864 (a futex wake), then NO syscalls for ~40,000 lines.
- No futex wait-begin and no TBEPW for pid=14 after 20864 -> it is NOT in a futex or epoll wait (those log for tgid=14).
- So pid=14 is either (A) spinning in userspace (running, no syscalls) or (B) blocked in some non-futex/non-epoll
  syscall that left no wait beacon, or (C) runnable-but-not-scheduled. STILL UNDISTINGUISHED — the census that would
  have told us was pointed at the wrong process.
