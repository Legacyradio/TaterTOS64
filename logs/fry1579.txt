WHAT IT IS (fry1578): pid=14 is in a userspace busy-loop whose syscalls are FUTEX_WAIT (nr=202) returning -11 EAGAIN,
interleaved with stat(ENOENT) + epoll_ctl(EEXIST). The TBSPIN kernel-PC sampler caught it 156x at cs=0x08 in

between pid=14's userspace read and the kernel's recheck:
   uaddr=6fbfee01e950 expected=26 actual=29   (a counter/seq racing ahead)

63ad6e0 DOES complete (jsc-wait-return rc=0). So it is these specific contended locks pid=14 cannot win.
