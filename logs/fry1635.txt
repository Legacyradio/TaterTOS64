WHY (Rule 6)

- ROOT CAUSE (confirmed in code, not DeepSeek's logs): DeepSeek (fry1606-1634, the "Rules 1/5/6/75" logs)

  known slow demand-paging grind (fry1586 + the Linux-compat handoff: the "freeze" is page-in, NOT a
  deadlock; TBDPF cr2 advances per fault). The one genuinely-blocking item is chrome_probe, which
  fry_wait()s on Chromium paging in BEFORE the GUI launches.

- src/user/init/init.c: replaced DeepSeek's forced-shell block with the GUI launch loop restored verbatim

  WHY-comment to reflect the real reason (not "NVMe size bump").
- gui.fry IS built (159KB) and staged to /system/GUI.FRY + /GUI.FRY by build_iso.sh (lines 327/354/576);
  paths match init.c gui_paths[] -> the binary was present all along, just never launched.
