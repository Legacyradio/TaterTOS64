================================================================================
WHAT THE CLAUDE TUI "DISASTER" ACTUALLY WAS (root cause, confirmed)
================================================================================
- NOT the column width (winsize correctly reports 120x36; font advance 8px == TERM_CHAR_W).
- Cause A: font8x16 was [128] (ASCII only) and gfx_draw_char did `if (c>127) c='?'`. Claude's
  TUI uses UTF-8 box-drawing/blocks/symbols (3 bytes each) -> every byte became '?' = garbage.
- Cause B: term_render drew every cell in ONE gray (0xD0D0D0) -> no color.
