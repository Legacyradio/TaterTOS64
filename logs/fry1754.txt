WHY RAM-backed ToTFS (not a new FS, not a disk /tmp):
  - totfs_mount(fs, BD, part_lba) already takes ANY block device -> point ToTFS at RAM.
  - Reuses 100% of ToTFS (mount/alloc/read/write/dir ops + the multi-block-bitmap
    scaling from fry1752). Temp lives in RAM -> cleared each boot, no disk wear.
  - Disk /tmp (mkdir on NVMe) was the band-aid; rejected (persists, wears the drive).
