ROOT CAUSE (corrected, final): the boot crawl was NOT the buddy allocator
(fry1773 was wrong — that was ~14%) and NOT the mmap churn itself. It was the
SERIAL VM-EXIT TAX: under KVM every byte to the emulated UART is a VM-exit, and
the "VM COMMIT OK" diagnostic fired on every mmap (2927x, 291 KB). The
instrumentation studying the churn WAS the bottleneck. Removing it = 12x less
serial = fast.

LESSON: stop optimizing the thing that's loud in the logs; the loudness (serial
I/O) can itself be the cost under virtualization. Measure serial volume early.
