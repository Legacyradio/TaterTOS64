=== BUILD-ORDER LESSON RE-LEARNED THIS STEP (I got it wrong once) ===
After the fry1806 acceptance-test `touch src/include/unistd.h`, posix.o became
out of date. Running `make -f Python3.mk python3` FIRST failed with
  "[<builtin>: src/user/libc/posix.o] fatal error: stdint.h: No such file"
because Python3.mk has no rule for libc objects and make fell back to its
built-in rule. THE ORDER IS NOT OPTIONAL:
    1) MAIN Makefile  -> all libc objects (incl. gap_stubs.o AND rust_compat.o)
    2) Python3.mk     -> python3 relink
    3) build_iso.sh
The artifact was NOT compromised — python3.fry at 23:53:15 came from the earlier
PY_EXIT=0 FULL rebuild that already contained both source edits, and that exact
file (15,820,128 bytes) was what got staged. The tree was afterwards reconciled
properly (LIBC_EXIT=0, PY_EXIT=0, `make -n` pending = 0) and the relink produced
a byte-identical-size binary, then the ISO was rebuilt so ordering is clean.
