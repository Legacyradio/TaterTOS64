=======================================
GCC 15 EXHAUSTIVENESS FIXES
=======================================

GCC 15 is stricter than clang about exhaustive switch coverage. It
warns -Werror=return-type when a switch covers all enum values but
has no explicit default/return after the switch block. Fixed so far:

  base/base64.cc           — default: __builtin_unreachable()
  base/metrics/histogram.cc — __builtin_unreachable() after switch
  base/metrics/sparse_histogram.cc — removed 4 redundant std::move()
  base/process/current_process.cc — 2x __builtin_unreachable()
  base/task/common/operations_controller.cc — __builtin_unreachable()
  base/task/common/task_annotator.cc — __builtin_unreachable()
  base/task/sequence_manager/thread_controller.cc — 2x __builtin_unreachable()
  base/task/task_traits.cc — __builtin_unreachable()
  base/task/thread_pool/task_tracker.cc — 3x __builtin_unreachable()
  base/threading/hang_watcher.cc — __builtin_unreachable()
